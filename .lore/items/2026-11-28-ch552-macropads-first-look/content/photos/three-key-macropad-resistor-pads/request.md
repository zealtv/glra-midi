# Photo: 3 key macropad unpopulated resistor pads

Bob sent this photo on 2026-11-28 and asked: “For the same repo - point out the unpopulated resistor pads.” The intended repository is glra-midi. This is another view of the 3 key macropad board, showing three switch positions in a column.

Please note the four unpopulated resistor footprints beside the CH552G chip, marked **R10, R12, R16, and R17** on the silkscreen. They form the two lower rows of the 2-column footprint group; the upper row is marked C2 and C4. The photo also shows two unpopulated footprints nearer the USB-C connector, apparently marked D2 and D3; Bob specifically called out the resistor pads.
